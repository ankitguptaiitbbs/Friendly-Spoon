# 🟦 Four Sticks, One Square

> *A tiny story about equality, constraints, and why symmetry is unforgiving.*

---

## 📦 The Setup

Four sticks arrive at the gate:

* Stick **A** 🪵
* Stick **B** 🪵
* Stick **C** 🪵
* Stick **D** 🪵

They are rigid. No bending. No breaking. No cheating.

Their dream? **To become a square.**

---

## 🧠 The Rule of the World

This universe has exactly **one law**:

> A square exists **if and only if** all four sides are equal.

Not *almost* equal.
Not *two pairs* equal.
Not *close enough*.

All four. Or nothing.

---

## ⚖️ The Trial

The sticks line up.
They are measured.
Compared.
Silently judged.

The judge asks only one question:

```text
Are you all the same length?
```

No angles are checked.
No geometry is drawn.
No construction begins.

Because if equality fails here,
**everything else is wasted effort.**

---

## 🔁 The Loop of Fate

This trial repeats **t times**.
Each time, a new group of four steps forward.

Some pass.
Some fail.

There is no appeal.

---

## ✨ The Decision Logic

The verdict is brutally simple:

* If `A == B == C == D` → **YES** ✅
* Otherwise → **NO** ❌

No narration.
No apology.
Just truth.

---

## 🧩 The Code Behind the Curtain

```cpp
string msg = (a==b && c==d && a==c) ? "YES" : "NO";
```

A single expression.
A single idea.

Equality collapses the problem.

---

## 🟩 When It Works

Four equal sticks.
Four equal sides.
Four right angles waiting to exist.

The square snaps into reality.

Symmetry achieved. 🟩✨

---

## 🟥 When It Doesn’t

One stick too long.
One too short.

The shape never forms.
The system refuses to lie.

Constraints win. 🟥

---

## 🧠 Moral (for Engineers)

* Check **invariants** early
* Kill impossible states fast
* Geometry starts with **equality**, not imagination

Or put differently:

> If the sides don’t match,
> don’t bother drawing the square.

---

🧱 *Four sticks entered.*
🟦 *Only equal ones became a square.*
